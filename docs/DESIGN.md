# Landing page design notes

Design Read: landing page for an open-source Android app, for tablet owners. Language: ink on paper with one accent. ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 3, MOTION 1. Mode: during (rules applied while building).

- The black bar at the top and the black stage under the hero are the notch itself, so the page borrows the product's own shape.
- Real screenshots from the author's tablet, masked to the notch outline. No invented UI mock.
- Colors: paper #f4f1ea, ink #14120f, one accent #b8420a (about 5:1 on paper), black only for the screen areas.
- Type: Archivo (extended width) for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text, with system fallbacks.
- Layout: left-aligned, rows with rules instead of cards, no bento grid, no eyebrow badge, no arrows, no em dashes.
- Motion: none beyond a hover color change, which respects reduced motion.
- Honesty: measured numbers say where they come from and that they are one device; known limits get their own section.
- Footer is one line. Every link points to a real destination.
