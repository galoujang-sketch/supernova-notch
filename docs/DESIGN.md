# Landing page design notes

Rebuilt from zero to avoid resembling other notch apps.

- Concept: a poster. Full-bleed cobalt hero, one live black notch hanging from the very top edge, a huge wordmark under it, and a numbered feature index that drives the notch.
- Palette: cobalt #2a3cf5, ink #0b0b10, paper #f2efe6, one orange #ff8a3d for small signals.
- Type: Archivo at extended width and heavy weight for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text.
- Rhythm: cobalt, paper, black, paper, cobalt, paper, so each section changes color instead of repeating cards.
- Real screenshots are shown as black strips that hang from their own top edge, the way the notch does.
- Honesty: measured numbers say where they come from; "Not finished" lists the known limits.
- The donation section exists at the end of the page and stays hidden until a link is set in the script.
- Motion: the hero notch cycles through its states and stops on hover or click; a slow ticker runs under the hero. Both stop with reduced motion.
