Everything useful, in one place

A small notch at the top of the screen that grows when you need it and gets out of the way when you don't.

♪

Music with synced lyrics

Cover art, title and controls. Lyrics run along the top strip and pass behind the notch like karaoke.

▭

Clean top bar

While music plays, the clock, notifications, Wi-Fi and battery move into the notch. When it stops, the normal bar returns.

⏱

Focus timer

Focus and break durations you can set to match your routine.

▦

Calendar and water

A week calendar and a water tracker right in the panel.

⚡

Quick HUD pop-ups

Charger plugged or unplugged, headphones connected, volume and lost Wi-Fi.

✦

Nova, the mascot

When idle, the notch shows a tiny night sky with an occasional supernova, then freezes to save battery.

Also: auto-hides in fullscreen apps, English and Indonesian interface.

Install in 3 steps

No PC and no account needed.

  • Download and install the APKAllow installs from unknown sources for your browser, then open the APK file.
  • Grant the permissionsDisplay over other apps, notification access, and Accessibility (recommended). On Android 13+, open App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings first. Also turn off battery optimization for the app.
  • Play some musicThe notch appears at the top center. Tap it to open the panel.

FAQ

Honest answers, including the limits.

Does it work on Android tablets other than Huawei?

Very likely on Android 8.0 and newer, but it has only been tested on a Huawei MatePad 11.5 (HarmonyOS 3.1). On other devices fullscreen detection or notch position may behave differently. Please report it on GitHub Issues.

How much RAM and battery does it use?

About 44 MB of RAM and 2% of the day's total battery consumption (around 6 hours of screen-on time with music), measured on a MatePad 11.5. That is one device and one day, so your numbers may differ.

Is it safe? What data is sent?

No accounts, ads or tracking. The Accessibility service does not read your screen or keystrokes. Network requests are only for lyrics (lrclib.net, sending song title, artist and duration) and, if your player provides a web link, the cover image. The code is open so you can check it.

Why is there a black strip at the top?

Android apps can't hide the status bar, only cover it. The strip only appears while music plays and fades out when it stops.

The notch disappears after a while

Turn off battery optimization for the app. Some Android skins close background apps aggressively.

The song cover doesn't show

Make sure notification access is on for Supernova Notch and the tablet is online. Toggle that permission off and on if needed, then change the song.

Is it free?

Yes. MIT license. Feel free to fork and change it.
